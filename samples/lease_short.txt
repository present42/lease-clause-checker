SAMPLE RESIDENTIAL LEASE (synthetic, written for this demo; not a real lease)

Unit 4, 120 Example Street, Toronto. Monthly rent: $2,150, due on the first day of each month.

1. A deposit of one month's rent is payable on signing and will be used as the rent for the final month.

2. Pets are not allowed in the apartment or the building.

3. On signing, the Tenant hands over a dated cheque for each month of the lease term.

4. If any rent payment is missed, the rent for the rest of the term becomes due at once.

5. On 24 hours' written notice giving the reason and a time between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m., the Landlord may come in to carry out repairs.

6. This lease ends automatically when the one-year term is over, and the Tenant must leave the unit on that day.

7. The Tenant is responsible for repairing any damage to the unit caused by the Tenant's own carelessness or that of the Tenant's guests.

8. Whenever rent is a month or more overdue, the Landlord may seize the Tenant's belongings and hold them until it is paid.
