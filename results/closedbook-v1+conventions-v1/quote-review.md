The categories, and the order they are tried in, are defined in [quotediff.py](../../src/lease_clause_checker/quotediff.py). They describe wording only: a cosmetic difference is not a wrong statement of the law, and whether a changed word changes the meaning is for the hand check.

Hand checks are kept in [quote-hand-check.csv](quote-hand-check.csv): fill in `hand_check` with `agree` or the category you would give instead (one of `conventions`, `cosmetic`, `wrong_cite`, `meaning_changed`, `not_in_act`, `no_quote`, `too_short`, `heading`), add a note if useful, and regenerate. The categories have not yet been checked by hand.

In the **quote vs the Act** column, `[-…-]` marks the Act's words that the quote drops or replaces and `{+…+}` the quote's own words; the rest is in both. It runs from the quote's first matched word to its last, in the subsection named under **lined up with**: the cited section's, or another section's for a wrong citation. A quote that lines up with nothing in the Act is shown as given.
